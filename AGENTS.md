# Development

Product planning is pending. Do not infer product requirements from the repository name.
Before implementation, agree a scoped specification with observable acceptance criteria.
Use isolated branches/worktrees, preserve unrelated work and submit changes through PRs.
Keep verification evidence attached to the change. Infrastructure CI is not product validation.

Workflow, ownership and policy changes require code-owner review. Do not bypass checks,
change their acceptance criteria, add credentials or enable deployment without explicit authority.
